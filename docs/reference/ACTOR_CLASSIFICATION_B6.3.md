# B6.3 — Approved Actor Classification

## Canon and implementation scope

- All 15 current playable characters are classified as `organic` by default, per user-approved canon.
- Armor, shields, weapons, and ordinary equipment do not alter actor composition.
- Galloz is `organic` and additionally tagged `electronic-systems` to represent his cybernetic body components and EMP susceptibility.
- Fully android characters (for example, future G.A.T. android GDB-V1) are exceptions and must be classified from confirmed lore when introduced; no such character is added in this milestone.
- The suggested full-Serpent-team Toxic resistance bonus is retained as a future idea only. It is not implemented or populated in B6.3.

## Current playable classifications

Falcon, Phantom, and Serpent playable roster actors receive `organic`. Galloz receives `organic` + `electronic-systems`. Enemy classifications remain unchanged; the four previously confirmed mechanical enemy archetypes retain `mechanical` + `electronic-systems`.

## Constraints

Capabilities remain inert metadata. This milestone does not activate susceptibility behavior, assign affinity values, implement faction bonuses, alter stats/skills/damage/status/AI/RNG, or change golden transcripts. `electronic-systems` is a descriptive capability; its combat interactions are not activated here.
