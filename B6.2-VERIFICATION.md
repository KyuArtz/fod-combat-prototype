## Scope

- Added `systems/factionAffinities.js` schema validators for known faction keys, damage resistance entries, effect resistance entries, control resistance percentage, control duration reduction, and actor partial affinity overrides.
- Added `data/factionAffinities.js` with empty profiles for Falcon, Phantom, and Serpent.
- Actor factory exposes validated `affinityOverrides`, defaulting to `{}`.

## Schema contract

- Damage and effect resistance percentages must be finite and in the inclusive 0–100 range.
- Optional filters must be arrays of non-empty strings; duplicates are normalized.
- Control resistance percentage is 0–100.
- Control duration reduction must be a non-negative integer.
- Faction keys are restricted to Falcon, Phantom, and Serpent IDs.
- Actor override profiles are partial and validated. Proposed inheritance/override resolution is documented but not implemented.

## Deliberately not changed

- No faction affinity values or resistance magnitudes are populated.
- No runtime merging or affinity resolution exists.
- No character/enemy capabilities, skills, combat formula, susceptibility, AI, RNG, or golden transcripts were changed.
